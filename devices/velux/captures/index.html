    Raw RX captures — Velux BG-RC011-02 (KLI 31x) + window actuator

    Frames sniffed with an SX1262 (Heltec V4.2) running this repo's ESPHome component in receive mode. Radio config (confirmed correct, see ../velux-frame-analysis.md §1): 868.95 MHz, 2-FSK, 38.4 kbps, 19.2 kHz deviation, NRZ, no shaping, sync word 57 FD 99, 512-bit preamble, radio CRC off, fixed-length RX widened to 64 bytes.

    How to read a line: rssi=<dBm> then the 64 bytes the radio delivered for that packet, MSB-first as RadioLib hands them over. A frame starts at byte 0 (the sync word is consumed by the radio) and ends where the trailing preamble run (55/AA/FF) begins; everything after that is the next frame's preamble or post-frame idle, not payload.

    Devices seen (source address / device-id bytes at idx8-9): - 7E 51 D2 / F4 8F — the window wall remote - 7E 46 94 / AD 31 — a second (identical) remote - 7E 5A 11 / 35 29 — a third remote (appeared during an anlern attempt) - actuator (destination) is 10 04 01 (a 10 05 01 variant also appears)

    Settled. These looked like they validated no CRC and ran to ~48 bytes because they still carried their UART start/stop framing (ten bits per byte; docs/radio.md). De-framed, all 173 validate CRC-16/KERMIT and decode to standard frames, one of them a 1W key transfer. The de-framer is src/protocol/iohome_phy_framing.{h,cpp}, host-tested against these very bytes in test/test_phy_framing/. See ../velux-frame-analysis.md §0.
